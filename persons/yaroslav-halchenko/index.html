Yaroslav Halchenko

Founding Director of the Center for Open Neuroscience. Research Professor of Psychological and Brain Sciences and Adjunct Professor of Computer Science at Dartmouth College. Official Debian Developer and open source contributor leading DANDI, DataLad, PyMVPA, and NeuroDebian projects. Serves on BIDS and NWB technical advisory committees.
