Content-neutral engine and deterministic runtime for metadata-driven sites. A site's content lives as records in a store; the engine projects them into the pages that get served, so the records stay the single source of truth. This website is built that way. Derived from ORINOCO, the Psychoinformatics group's research information infrastructure, and lighter by design.
