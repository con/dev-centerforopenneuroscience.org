svgtune generates a set of SVG files from a single (Inkscape) drawing by toggling the visibility, style or placement of its layers and groups, as directed by a short script -- the way to build incremental slides, or to extract a panel of a poster that is regenerated whenever the poster changes. Written in 2009 and packaged for Debian, it was revived in 2026 with %only, %prune and %crop directives and a test suite.
