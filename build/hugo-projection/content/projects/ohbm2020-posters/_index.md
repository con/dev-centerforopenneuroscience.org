A centralized registry of posters and their Jitsi rooms for OHBM 2020, the first edition held entirely online. The conference had no working way for an attendee to find a poster and walk up to its author; this gave it one, as a browsable catalogue backed by a DataLad dataset. Archived since 2022: it did its job for the meeting it was built for.
