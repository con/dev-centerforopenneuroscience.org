The technology research and development project within ReproNim that YOH leads at Dartmouth. In practice it is the tooling that makes a study reproducible end to end: capturing what the scanner and the stimulus computer actually did, converting it into standard layouts without a human in the loop, and re-running the analysis in a recorded environment.
